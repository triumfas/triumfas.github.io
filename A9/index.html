A9

A9 is a fast app-search pop-up for Android 16+. It opens as a compact panel on top of whatever you are doing, finds an app or contact with a T9 keypad in a few taps, and offers useful actions on long-press.

Features

  • T9 search by name, words, initials and package name; matched letters are highlighted.
  • Recent and most-used apps first; pin or hide any app.
  • Long-press menu: App info, Add to Home screen, Hide, Play Store page, Uninstall, Force stop.
  • Optional contact search, Quick Launch notification and icon packs.
  • Customizable look: theme, colors, transparency, window position and size.

Privacy first

A9 has no internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no accounts. Everything stays on your device. Read the Privacy Policy and the permissions explained.
